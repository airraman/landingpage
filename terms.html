Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 7, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to DeepWork. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the DeepWork mobile application ("App").

By using DeepWork, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • No Account Information: DeepWork does not require you to create an account, so we do not collect registration information such as name, email, or password.
  • Contact Information: If you contact us for support via email, we collect your email address and any information you choose to provide in your message.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Activity and Usage Data: We collect data about your work sessions, including session duration, start/end times, and productivity metrics to provide core App functionality.
  • Device Information: We may collect information about your device, including device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • App Performance Data: We collect technical data such as crash reports, error logs, and performance metrics to improve App stability and functionality.

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • User-Generated Content: The App does not allow users to create, upload, or share content (text, images, videos, etc.).
  • Location Data: We do not collect precise or approximate location information.
  • Camera or Microphone Access: We do not access your device's camera or microphone.
  • Contacts or Photos: We do not access your contacts, photos, or other personal files on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Core Functionality: To provide time tracking, activity monitoring, and productivity analytics features
  • Service Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve App performance and user experience
  • Technical Support: To respond to your support requests and troubleshoot technical issues
  • App Stability: To identify and fix bugs, crashes, and technical problems
  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with the App and which features are most valuable

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

Most of your productivity data is stored locally on your device. This means:

  • Your activity history and metrics remain on your device
  • Data is not automatically backed up to our servers
  • If you uninstall the App, local data will be deleted

3.2 Cloud Storage (if applicable)

If you subscribe to premium features that include cloud backup:

  • Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information
  • Data is stored on secure servers provided by reputable cloud service providers

3.3 Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit using SSL/TLS
  • Secure storage practices
  • Regular security assessments
  • Limited access to personal data by our team

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4.1 We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

These service providers are contractually obligated to keep your information confidential and use it only for the purposes we specify.

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to:

  • Valid legal processes (subpoenas, court orders)

4.4 Business Transfers

If DeepWork is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change and provide information about your choices.

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Access and Control

  • Access Your Data: Since most data is stored locally on your device, you can access it directly through the App
  • Delete Your Data: You can delete your local data by uninstalling the App or using in-app data deletion features
  • Opt-Out of Analytics: You may be able to opt out of certain analytics tracking through App settings

5.2 Subscription Data

For subscription-related data held by app stores, you must contact the respective app store (Apple or Google) to exercise your rights.

5.3 Communication Preferences

If you have contacted us via email, you can request that we delete your contact information from our support records.

6. Data Retention
